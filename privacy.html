Train Builder Kids

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

No data collected No ads No internet access

Train Builder Kids (“the app”) is a game for children made by Fast Hub Games. We built it so that it collects nothing about anyone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on our servers, transmit or share any personal or non-personal information. That includes names, email addresses, device identifiers, advertising IDs, location, photos, contacts, usage analytics and crash reports.

No internet access

The app works fully offline. The released app does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send or receive data over the network.

What stays on the device

The app saves game progress (coins, stars, trains built, stickers, sound settings) in the app’s own private storage on the device. This information never leaves the device. Android cloud backup is turned off for this app, so progress is not copied to any cloud account. Uninstalling the app, or using For Parents → Reset all progress, deletes it.

No ads, no third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking or data-collection software. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no links that a child can follow out of the app.

Children

The app is designed for children. Because it collects no personal information, no personal information from children is collected, used or disclosed. This is consistent with the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU GDPR and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code. Settings and progress reset are behind a parent gate (a simple maths question).

In-app purchases

The app offers one optional, one-time purchase (the "Full Train Pack"), shown only in the grown-ups area behind a maths question. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. The app never sees or stores payment details. It only keeps a "pack owned" flag on the device, and it asks the store app on the device whether the pack was bought, so it can be restored on a new phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
